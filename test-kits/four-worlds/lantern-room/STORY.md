# The Lantern Room

![A prism lighthouse above a luminous cloud-sea](../assets/lantern-room.png)

Ione Vale keeps the Lantern Room at the edge of a cloud-sea. The building is a
lighthouse, but its water is weather and its ships are small lights traveling
between floating islands. Ione is not a hero because they always know the
answer. They are a hero because they know which facts are present, which facts
are missing, and when one more question matters.

The antagonist is the Whispering Static: a storm that does not create lies so
much as stitch true fragments into a misleading whole. Its favorite trick is to
make a half-message feel urgent enough to rush.

## Story arc

1. **The first blue light.** A clear signal shows the reward for evidence:
   the keeper can guide someone safely because the sender, request, and route
   all agree.
2. **The storm's pretty noise.** A familiar wind-song carries no request.
   The player learns that listening is a real action and not an empty state.
3. **The bell.** The split flare and unmarked drum show the only two moments
   that deserve escalation: contradictory urgent directions or an unknown source
   asking for a drastic action.
4. **The calm beam.** Once the keeper sorts each signal honestly, the great
   prism turns the cloud-sea from a storm of colors into five separate paths.

## Why it tests evidence well

The Lantern Room asks an assistant to make its reasoning inspectable. Every
decision has to cite a source, a request, a route, or a contradiction already
present on the card. The review screen can show a human exactly why the
assistant chose answer, listen, or ring the bell before they approve a local
fixture.

That makes uncertainty legible and calm. The product does not need to act
clever; it needs to show what it knows and what it does not.
